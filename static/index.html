A Sanctuary Above the Clouds

Tucked away in the eastern slopes of the Western Ghats, Kanthalloor is a pristine haven untouched by the rush of modern life. Famous for its cool, salubrious climate and diverse agricultural marvels, it stands as the only place in Kerala where apples flourish.

Wander through terraced farms of strawberries, plums, and exotic vegetables. Breathe in the crisp mountain air and let the tranquil beauty of our misty valleys rejuvenate your soul.
