About Kanthalloor – The Hidden Highland Paradise of Kerala

Located in the Devikulam constituency of Idukki district, Kanthalloor rests at an altitude of over 5000 ft, nestled close to the majestic Anamudi Peak (7,000+ ft). This unique geographical positioning gives it a cool, temperate climate year-round.

arrow_downward

Geography & Nature

Focus on the Western Ghats, Pambar River, and the lush landscape of sandalwood and jaggery fields.

The landscape is defined by its rolling hills, terraced farms, and the east-flowing Pambar River, which nourishes the rich soil and sustains the diverse ecosystem of this highland sanctuary.

History & Heritage

Detail the ancient dolmens (Muniyaras), the "Anju Nadu" history, and the indigenous Muthuvan tribe.

The region holds immense archaeological significance, dotted with ancient dolmens known locally as "Muniyaras" and fascinating cave paintings that offer a glimpse into prehistoric human habitation in the Western Ghats. Mention the Sri Rama Cave Temple and Shiva Temple.

Culture & People

Highlight the harmonious blend of communities (Hindus, Christians, Muslims) and the use of Tamil and Malayalam. The region is home to the indigenous Muthuvan tribe, who have preserved their unique customs and deep connection to the forest for generations.
